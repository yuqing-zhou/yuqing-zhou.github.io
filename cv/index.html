Selected Research Projects

  • 2024
    Causally Calibrated Robust Classifier (CCR)
    • Developed a group-label-free framework for worst-group robustness under distribution shift using causal feature selection and inverse propensity weighting.
    • Achieved 70.7% worst-group accuracy on CivilComments, outperforming competing methods that require explicit group labels.
  • 2023 - 2024
    Shortcut Learning Analysis in Language Models
    • Developed a taxonomy and controlled benchmarks for occurrence-, style-, and concept-based shortcuts.
    • Evaluated BERT and Llama-family models, showing that scale alone does not consistently improve shortcut robustness.
  • 2023
    Generalized Propensity Learning for Post-Click Conversion Rate Prediction
    • Developed a generalized propensity-learning framework that reduces bias and variance in post-click conversion prediction.
    • Improved performance by 7% in DCG@2 and 6% in Recall@2 on the Yahoo dataset.
